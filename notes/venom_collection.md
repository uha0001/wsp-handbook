# Why Collect Venom?

Venom can be analyzed to study:

- Protein composition
- Peptide diversity
- Gene expression
- Evolution of venom systems
- Functional activity

---

# Sample Preservation

Proper preservation is essential because venom components can degrade over time.

Depending on the downstream application, samples may be:

- Frozen
- Stored in RNAlater
- Processed immediately

---

# Downstream Applications

Collected venom may be used for:

- Mass spectrometry
- RNA sequencing
- Functional assays
- Comparative studies
