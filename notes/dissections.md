# Goals

A successful dissection should:

- Recover intact venom glands
- Minimize contamination
- Preserve RNA quality
- Produce consistent samples

---

# General Considerations

- Work efficiently.
- Keep tissues cold whenever possible.
- Use clean instruments.
- Avoid damaging the venom gland.

---

# Sample Handling

After isolation, tissues may be:

- Snap frozen
- Stored in RNAlater
- Processed immediately

The handling method depends on the downstream experiment.

---

# Common Challenges

- Locating the venom gland
- Avoiding contamination from surrounding tissues
- Preventing tissue damage
- Maintaining RNA integrity
