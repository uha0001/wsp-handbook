# Venom Gland Dissection

> **Status:** Draft protocol based on laboratory observations and to be
> Verified during training.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Objective

Isolate venom glands while minimizing tissue damage and RNA degradation
for downstream RNA extraction and transcriptome sequencing.

# Materials

-   Live ants
-   Ice
-   Stereo/dissecting microscope
-   Fine forceps
-   RNase-free microcentrifuge tubes
-   RNA Shield
-   RNase-free workspace

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Observed Workflow

## Preparation

-   Label collection tubes before beginning.
-   Add RNA Shield to the collection tubes.
-   Immobilize ants on ice.
-   Prepare clean dissecting tools.
-   Set up the stereomicroscope.

## Dissection

-   Remove the head.
-   Open the abdomen.
-   Remove surrounding tissues.
-   Locate the venom gland in the third abdominal segment.
-   Carefully isolate the venom gland without rupturing it.
-   Perform the dissection dry (not submerged in solution).

## Sample Preservation

-   Immediately transfer the isolated venom gland into RNA Shield.
-   Place the remaining body tissues into a separate labeled tube.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Goals

A successful dissection should:

-   Recover intact venom glands.
-   Minimize contamination from surrounding tissues.
-   Preserve RNA quality.
-   Produce consistent samples between individuals.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Critical Points

-   Keep specimens cold before dissection.
-   Minimize the time tissues remain at room temperature.
-   Transfer glands into RNA Shield immediately after isolation.
-   Avoid rupturing the venom gland.
-   Use clean, RNase-free instruments.
-   Maintain a consistent workflow across all samples.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# Current Observations

-   Ants are immobilized directly on ice.
-   Dissections are performed under a stereomicroscope.
-   The venom gland is isolated before being transferred into RNA
    Shield.
-   Remaining body tissues are preserved separately for future analyses.
